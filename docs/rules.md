# LunchDesk Business Rules

## Decisions

- **Order cutoff:** 10:00 AM on the meal day
- **Cancellation:** Cancelling before the cutoff is free. After the cutoff, the meal cost is charged, with no refund
- **QR code:** Valid for 1 minute
- **Subscription:** Gives system access and a Rs. 1000 balance
- **Advance ordering:** Employees can order up to 7 days ahead
- **Weekends:** No menu on weekends

## Open Question

When the Rs. 1000 runs out, do employees re-subscribe or top up any amount?

**My answer:** Employees top up any amount (minimum Rs. 100) once their balance runs out, so they don't have to pay for a full new subscription.
